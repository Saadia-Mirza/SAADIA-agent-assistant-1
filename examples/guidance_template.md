## 1. Purpose

This guidance document establishes the requirements for managing organisational
data assets in accordance with applicable regulations and best practices. All
personnel who handle data shall adhere to the principles set out herein.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors
who collect, process, store, or transmit data on behalf of the organisation.

## 3. Definitions and Acronyms

- **Data Asset**: Any information, in any format, that has value to the
  organisation.
- **PII**: Personally Identifiable Information — any data that can be used
  to identify an individual.
- **DPA**: Data Protection Authority.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role              | Responsibility                                         |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Data Owner        | Ensure data is classified and protected appropriately  |
| Data Custodian    | Implement technical controls as directed by the Owner  |
| All Staff         | Follow this guidance and report potential breaches     |

## 5. Policy Statements

5.1 All data assets shall be classified according to the Data Classification
Standard prior to storage or transmission.

5.2 Access to data shall be granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed
at least annually.

5.3 Data breaches shall be reported to the Data Protection Authority within
72 hours of discovery, consistent with applicable regulations.

## 6. Procedures

Refer to the Data Management Procedures Manual for step-by-step guidance on
implementing the requirements above.
