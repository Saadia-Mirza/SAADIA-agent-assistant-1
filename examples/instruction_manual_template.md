# Employee Self-Service Portal — User Guide

## Introduction

This guide explains how to use the Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal to
manage your personal information, request leave, and view payslips.

## Prerequisites / Before You Begin

- You must have an active company account.
- You need access to the corporate intranet or VPN.
- Supported browsers: Chrome 120+, Firefox 118+, Edge 118+.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Open your browser and navigate to `https://ess.company.internal`.
2. Enter your **username** (your company email address).
3. Enter your **password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

> **NOTE**: If you have forgotten your password, click **Forgot Password**
> on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

## Updating Personal Information

### Changing Your Address

1. Click your **name** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **My Profile** from the dropdown menu.
3. Click **Edit** next to the *Address* section.
4. Enter your new address in the fields provided.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

<!-- PLACEHOLDER: My Profile — Address Section -->
*Figure: Address fields in the My Profile screen.*

### Changing Your Emergency Contact

1. From **My Profile**, scroll down to the *Emergency Contacts* section.
2. Click **Add Contact** or **Edit** next to an existing contact.
3. Complete all required fields.
4. Click **Save**.

## Requesting Leave

1. Click **Leave** in the left-hand navigation panel.
2. Click **New Request**.
3. Select the **Leave Type** from the dropdown (e.g., Annual, Sick, Parental).
4. Choose the **Start Date** and **End Date** using the date pickers.
5. Enter a **Reason** (optional for Annual Leave; required for other types).
6. Click **Submit Request**.

<!-- PLACEHOLDER: Leave Request Form -->
*Figure: The New Leave Request form.*

> **IMPORTANT**: Leave requests must be submitted at least **3 business days**
> before the intended start date, unless it is sick leave.

## Viewing Payslips

1. Click **Payroll** in the left-hand navigation panel.
2. Click **Payslips**.
3. Select the desired month from the list.
4. Click **Download PDF** to save a copy.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                        | Solution                                                  |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Cannot log in                  | Verify your credentials; contact IT if the issue persists |
| Leave request not submitted    | Check that all required fields are completed              |
| Payslip not available          | Payslips are published on the last working day of each month |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I access the portal from my mobile phone?**
A: Yes. The portal is mobile-responsive and works on any modern smartphone browser.

**Q: Who do I contact for payroll errors?**
A: Email `payroll@company.com` with your employee ID and a description of the issue.

## Glossary

- **ESS Portal**: Employee Self-Service Portal — the web application described in this guide.
- **VPN**: Virtual Private Network — required for remote access to internal systems.
